About Me

5 Years of Moderation & Community Building

I started moderating Minecraft servers at 14 years old. Over the past five years, I've spent extensive time learning how communities work from the staff side—handling player concerns, keeping chat constructive, and working side-by-side with fellow moderators and server owners.

Good community management isn't about arbitrary authority or rigid enforcement. It's about being fair, listening to players, understanding both sides of a conflict, and creating an environment where players feel welcome from their first join.

Through my work across MelonSMP, Hyrise, and FreshSMP, I've learned that consistent moderation and dependable staff teamwork are what turn a casual player base into a strong, lasting community.

— Peter (P3troleum)
